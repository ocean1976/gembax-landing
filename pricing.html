Common Questions

What data do I need to provide?

You'll fill out a guided intake form with questions about your operations — revenue, headcount, key processes, and pain points. No financial statements or sensitive data required. The more detail you share, the sharper the analysis.

How long does it take?

Scan reports are delivered within 5 business days. Full Diagnosis reports within 7–10 business days after intake completion. Transformation timelines are scoped per engagement.

What industries do you cover?

Manufacturing, services, retail, logistics, technology/SaaS, and sales operations. Anywhere there's a value stream, there's waste to find.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We use encrypted connections, don't share your business data with third parties, and you can request deletion at any time. See our Privacy Policy.

What's the difference between Scan and Diagnosis?

Scan gives you the top 3 waste areas with dollar estimates. Diagnosis goes deeper — all 7+1 categories, bottleneck detection, Pareto analysis, and a full 90-day action plan with ROI projections.
